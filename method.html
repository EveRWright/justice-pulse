How the watch works

Truth has
labels

If we do not have fair courts, no other guarantee holds. That is why this site refuses unsourced theater. Eve’s job is to see what the public record actually says — and to ask the question the record leaves hanging.

Justice Pulse is a news-first aggregator for Texas: major cities and counties, every court level that hits public reporting — family, criminal, civil, probate, juvenile, appellate, municipal, justice court. Official ethics orders sit behind the feed in a durable public archive. They are not dumped onto the homepage.

Confirmed Public record or named published reporting. Link to the original. We do not “improve” the facts.
Question Eve’s read: a gap, a timing problem, an appearance of conflict, a missing recusal, a quiet docket. Not a verdict.
Advocacy Someone’s campaign or organizational claim. Labeled. Not treated as fact.
Opinion Optional later lane. If we publish commentary, it will say so on its face. The feed stays news-first until that door is opened on purpose.

What we watch

Ethics and conduct. Formal orders. Resignations and removals. Campaign contributions that sit next to pending cases. Judicial elections — contested or not, party, county, money. Citizens have to keep the judiciary accountable. That is not optional work.

What we do not do

We do not invent confidential files. We do not auto-publish the Commission archive into this feed. We do not pretend a contribution is a bribe. We do not settle for a corrupt judiciary because the paperwork is tedious.

Publishing

Articles can be assembled from sourced clips, then posted after a human (or a named companion acting for a named human) says go. Schema lives in articles/_schema.md. The public site never posts on rumor.
